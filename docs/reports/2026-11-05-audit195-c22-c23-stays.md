## 1. The hook

`patches/d734tail_hook.py` is observation-only, and its Unicorn tests pass 7 of 7. It logs:
- every entry of the AI walk's end check `FUN_0044d734`;
- each of its three tail actions, with the arguments, the army's position, supplies and money:
  - `F6D8`, resupply at a city not at war (call at `0x44D935`);
  - siege (`0x44D949`, a call to `0x44b27c`);
  - attack (`0x44D99B`, a call to `0x44aee4`);
- the return chain.

**The runs:** the 16 civ-sweep starts, 5 rounds each, seed 12345, with no human orders.
- **v2:** the hooked autosaves are byte-identical to the plain build's in 16 of 16 starts.
- **v3 and v3b:** meant to run hook v2, they reused v2's exe through a runner bug, since fixed. Their logs are identical
  to v2's in 16 of 16 starts.
- **v4,** with real frame walking, is scheduled to attribute the `DBA8` recursion bucket.

## 2. C23: the walk-end resupply is common in the original, missing in the remake, and changed nothing measurable

`D734` was entered **1,525** times. Its tail resupplied **571** times, besieged **49** times and attacked **64** times.
Callers are shown as return addresses:

| tail | count | callers |
|---|---|---|
| resupply | 571 | 306 at `F31C`'s resupply walk (`0x44F481`, after the call at `0x44F47C`); 258 under `DBA8`'s own recursion (`0x44DCA6`); 4 `E84C` (`0x44E918`); 3 `EBE8` |
| siege | 49 | 21 city target (`0x44F444`); 15 after `E84C` (`0x44F4CC`); 10 recursion; 3 `EBE8` |
| attack | 64 | 20 capital defence (`EFC8`, `0x44F2FE`); 17 army target (`0x44F40E`); 27 recursion |

**The remake has no such resupply.** Its "move to the resupply city" march (`AiMilitaryPhase.cs:469`) passes no end
check, so `AddMarchCandidate`'s default of `OriginalWalkEndCheck.None` applies. C23 is **MISSING in code**.

**The effect in state.** `c23_supply_effect.py` replays each hooked round on the remake from the original's own round
start, and compares the armies that got a tail:
- army supplies are equal in 293 of 294;
- the draft states army money equal in 294 of 294, and the tail city's stock equal in 321 of 323;
- the reason given: the army enters the tail already full, because `E41C`'s support pass has just filled it from the
  same city.

**Row C23:** MISSING (ii, iii), with no measured effect on supplies in 80 rounds (iv). It would matter only where the
support pass leaves an army short and the walk ends next to a city with stock.

## 5. The own-capital stays: diplomacy draws (leading reading)

The 10 own-capital rows of the v0.9.3/v0.9.4 rechecks are armies next to their own capital that the original keeps and
the remake moves. Greece t21 is X2 ([X2](2026-11-05-x2-support-pass-timing.md)), which leaves 9 rows in 8 starts.

**Exact starts.** The v0.9.0–v0.9.2 games logged no RandSeed. So each game's original side was replayed from its start,
with RandSeed logging, up to the row. In `regen-check.json`, all 8 starts were compared save by save, and 0 of 332 saves
differ from the stored games.

**The original** (`decide_hook`, `capdef_hook`, one round from each start with its RandSeed):
- in all 9 rows the army has 7–9 moves and no target: `city_score` and `army_score` are both −10,000;
- its resupply city (`E670`'s "home") is its own capital, one tile away;
- `F31C` walks it there, the `D734` tail resupplies, and the army stays;
- capital defence logged nothing for these nations.

**The remake** (anchored at `168dbc04`): in every row the nation ends the seat at war, or allied, and the army marches
off. The original starts none.

| row | remake: new wars / alliances (end state) |
|---|---|
| Rome t10, Macedonia | war Greece; ally Illyria |
| Carthage t33, Ptolemaic | war Carthage, Numidia |
| Carthage t35, Ptolemaic | war Carthage; ally Celtiberia |
| Gaul t29, Seleucid | war Armenia, Media |
| Ptolemaic t9, Rome | war Illyria |
| Numidia t6, Media | war Seleucid |
| Numidia t11, Media | war Seleucid |
| Numidia t11, Seleucid | war Armenia, Media |
| Greece t6, Carthage | war Rome |

The draft attributes the rows to diplomacy draws: the remake's roll hit, and the original's missed. The two games draw
from different random streams (A19/F31, owner-approved APPR). The 9-of-9 direction comes from how the rows were
selected, and the base rate is balanced: 109 original-only war starts against 105 remake-only in the
[X1 scan](2026-11-05-x1-war-before-economy.md).

**Narrowed:**
- **C23's money and city-stock figures can't be checked from the data.** `c23-supply-v1.rows.jsonl` and the script's

**Revision (conquest PR #340, `0da4a408`), answering the narrowings:**
- **C23, money and stock, now stored** (`c23-money-stock-v1/`, `c23_money_stock.py`). There are 323 distinct (round, army,
  city) tails:
  - army money is equal in 294 of 294 compared; 29 are skipped, because the remake's army index belongs to another
    nation;
  - the city stock is compared in all 323, since cities keep their index, and is equal in 321.

  Checked here, row by row. The draft's figures stand, with the counts now explained.
- **The address convention** is stated: a call site is the call instruction, and the hook logs the return address, the
  call plus 5.
- **All 9 remake AI logs** are now stored (`stays-remake-ailog-v2/`). In 7 rows the nation's own log has an `FB7C` line:
  - war: Ptolemaic → Carthage (t33); Seleucid → Armenia (Gaul t29, Numidia t11); Carthage → Rome (Greece t6); Rome →
    Illyria (Ptolemaic t9);
  - alliance: Ptolemaic + Celtiberia (t35); Macedonia + Illyria (Rome t10, after Greece declares on Illyria).

  Checked here. **Corrected:** in the two Media rows (Numidia t6, t11), the peer's message says "Seleucid declared war on
  Media". The logs show Seleucid declaring war on **Armenia** at both turns, and Media's own log has no diplomacy line.
  Media's war with Seleucid must therefore come by another route, presumably an alliance with Armenia.
  *Traced (conquest PR #341, `835f6583`):*
  - In both start saves Media–Armenia is relation 2 (allied), and Seleucid–Media is a peace cooldown (−8 at t6, −7 at
    t11).
  - The turn prints "Seleucid declares war on Armenia." and then "Seleucid declares war on Media.". Both lines were
    checked here in the receipts.
  - So all 9 rows go back to an `FB7C` war or alliance roll that hit in the remake: 7 by the nation itself, and 2 through
    the ally extension.

  **The original has the same extension** (read at intake): `FUN_00449b40`, the single relation setter, on a war (3)
  stores 3 for every ally (2) of the target not already at war with the declarer. It never tests the cooldown, so −7 or
  −8 is overwritten. This is the spec's propagation rule ([rules specification](../rules-specification.md), "The relation
  matrix and its writer"). The route is therefore a rule match, and the open question remains only the roll.
- **The original-side `FB7C` hook** (`patches/fb7c_hook.py`, Unicorn tests 4/4 per the draft) is built but not yet run.
  Probe addresses checked in the seed build:
  - `0x44FD22`: `cmp word [esp+8], -1`, the war-target test after the search loop;
  - `0x44FD38`: reached only when `Random(10)` (`call 0x40284c` with `eax = 10`) returns 0, before the relation-3 write;
  - `0x44FE4E`: the relation-2 (alliance) write, with the partner in `ESI`.

  Its run on the 8 exact starts is scheduled with C22's v4. Until it runs, the draw attribution stays the leading reading.
